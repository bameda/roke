# Code of conduct

roke adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/)
as its code of conduct, with the additions below. Where they differ, the
additions apply.

It covers every space of the project: the repository, its issues, pull
requests and discussions, and any other channel where roke is discussed on
its behalf.

## Reporting an incident

Contact the maintainer, [@bameda](https://github.com/bameda), privately,
through the email address on his GitHub profile. Describe what happened,
where and when, with links if you have them. Reports are handled
confidentially, and you will get an answer as soon as possible.

If the incident involves the maintainer, say so in your report, and it will
be handled by someone else in the community whom you agree on.

The maintainer acts as the Community Moderators the Contributor Covenant
refers to, and applies its enforcement ladder.

## Our addition: complaints of "reverse" discrimination

Everyone is protected by this code, without exception. At the same time,
some people face exclusion in technical communities far more often than
others, and making roke welcoming to them is part of what this code is for.

So we will not act on reports whose only complaint is:

- that efforts to include under-represented or marginalised people
  discriminate against everyone else ("reverse" racism, sexism or any other
  form of discrimination);
- that someone set a reasonable boundary, such as asking not to be
  contacted or to be left out of a discussion;
- that someone pointed out or criticised behaviour they found
  discriminatory or exclusionary.

This does not exempt anyone from the rest of the code: setting a boundary or
criticising behaviour is not a licence to harass or insult, and every
report of harassment, personal attacks or discrimination is taken
seriously, whoever it comes from.
